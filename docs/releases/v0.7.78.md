# Discordmaxxer v0.7.78

This is attempt #2 at fixing widget skins not appearing on the full profile Board.

## What's changed

- The Board renderer now identifies each attached widget by its own label and content instead of selecting the surrounding profile or Board wrapper.
- Skin matching now walks deeper Board layouts, invalidates stale matches after widget recovery, and applies the selected skin immediately when the gallery choice changes.
- Apply feedback now reports whether visible widget cards were actually restyled instead of claiming success from a saved preference alone.
- The strict overlay and artifact checks confirm the corrected DMWidget renderer is included in the shipped Vencord bundle.

## Visibility boundary

Widget skins are Discordmaxxer-rendered presentation. They do not become native Discord widget fields for vanilla Discord users. After updating, reload Discordmaxxer once and reopen the profile Board to verify the card skin.
