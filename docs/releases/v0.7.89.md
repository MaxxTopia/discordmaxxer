# Discordmaxxer v0.7.89

This patch release improves the responsiveness and targeting of widget skins
when opening profiles and switching conversations.

## What's changed

- Profile Board and Playing-card skins now use a bounded visible-profile fast
  pass, with a short late-mount watcher for cards that Discord commits after
  the avatar/banner shell.
- The broad widget resolver is deferred to idle/background work and capped on
  the profile path, reducing the delay and normal-mode overhead when opening a
  profile.
- Repeated surface writes are skipped when nothing changed, preventing skin
  animations from restarting or flashing during profile updates.
- Switching between DMs now keeps the surface skin on only the current
  conversation row and header instead of leaving it on previously opened rows.
- Profile cards now resolve a viewed account's skin from that account's
  published widget-style marker. The viewer's local selection is no longer a
  fallback for another account, and profiles without a readable marker stay
  unskinned instead of borrowing the viewer's skin.

Widget skins remain Discordmaxxer-rendered enhancements and do not make these
visual effects appear in vanilla Discord.
