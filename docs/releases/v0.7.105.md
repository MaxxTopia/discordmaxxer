# Discordmaxxer v0.7.105

## Highlights

Restores widget skin gradient frames and borders around profile widget cards by rolling back an experimental DMWidget surface regression.

## What's New & Fixed

- **Widget Skins**: Fixed a regression introduced in v0.7.104 where colored gradient frames and borders around profile widget cards (such as Valorant and Fortnite cards) stopped rendering. Reverted DMWidget to the known-good v0.7.103 state to restore all card skin styling, borders, and animations.
