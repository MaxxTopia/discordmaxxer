# Discordmaxxer v0.7.77

This update fixes widget skins not appearing on the full profile Board.

## What's changed

- The Board renderer now finds the complete attached widget card instead of styling only a nested image or shallow wrapper.
- Valorant and Fortnite cards keep their selected skin when the profile Board opens, including cards whose application identity is exposed through deeper Board metadata.
- Discovery remains bounded and Discordmaxxer-only, so ordinary profile panels and unrelated Discord cards are left alone.

## Visibility boundary

Widget skins are Discordmaxxer-rendered presentation. They do not become native Discord widget fields for vanilla Discord users. After updating, reload Discordmaxxer once and reopen the profile Board to verify the card skin.
