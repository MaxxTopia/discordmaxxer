# Discordmaxxer v0.7.91

This release improves the profile, tier, and everyday-client experience while
keeping the free tier useful. The client remains Discordmaxxer-only: vanilla
Discord still cannot render Discordmaxxer banner, avatar, widget-skin, or
profile-flair enhancements.

## Highlights

- Free video playback now stays available to every tier, with saved URL limits
  of 1 for FREE, 5 for MAXXER, 20 for MAXXER+, and unlimited for MAXXER++.
- Free users keep custom presence text, optional Widget Mode, display-name
  looks, and right-click message translation without a helper app.
- DMProfileFlair now gives live banner, avatar, and theme-color previews, and
  the DMHub shortcut opens the actual profile-flair editor.
- The native DMTheme picker is easier to use, and profile/widget rendering
  keeps owner-specific flair and widget skins separate during delayed metadata.
- MAXXER and higher keep their tiered banner, avatar, theme-color, badge,
  name-shimmer, voice-color, beta, and saved-background benefits.
- The default install now includes 55 curated plugins (31 Vencord + 24
  Discordmaxxer), including the standalone DMTranslate plugin.
- Privacy, trim, typing, stream-mute, voice-keybind, beta, grant, badge, and
  tier-flair settings received compatibility and usability fixes.

## Important limits

- These visual features are rendered by Discordmaxxer. They do not make
  animated banners, profile flair, or widget skins appear in an unmodified
  vanilla Discord client.
- Cross-account animation, shared-roster delivery, and second-PC behavior
  still require a real recipient-client check after installation.
- Founder checkout terms remain the currently supported offer: $67 once, one
  month of MAXXER++ for the buyer, one one-month gift code, and a $12/month
  MAXXER++ price lock. A stronger Founder offer is not part of this release.
